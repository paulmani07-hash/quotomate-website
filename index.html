How it works

4 steps. 60 seconds. Done.

No apps to download. No software to learn. Just your phone and your voice.

1
Record
Hold your phone and describe the job — client name, location, work required, and total price.
"Quote for Sarah Johnson, replace hot water system at 14 River Street, total twelve hundred dollars..."
2
Send
Email the voice recording to your dedicated Quotomate address. Takes about 5 seconds.
3
Process
AI transcribes your voice, extracts all the details, calculates GST, and builds the quote automatically.
4
Receive
A professional branded PDF quote lands in your inbox within 60 seconds — ready to forward to your customer.
Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

One setup. Works automatically every single time.

Professional branded PDF
Your business name, ABN, address, and contact details on every quote. Looks like it came from a large company.
60-second turnaround
From voice message to PDF in your inbox. Faster than typing a single sentence.
Any phone, any device
Works on iPhone and Samsung Android. No special app needed — just the voice recorder you already use.
GST calculated automatically
GST is extracted and displayed automatically on every quote. No manual arithmetic on site.
Google Drive backup
Every quote saved automatically to your Google Drive Quotes folder. Never lose a quote again.
Auto quote numbering
Quotes are numbered sequentially and automatically. Looks organised and professional from day one.
Instant email delivery
PDF quote emailed to you automatically. Forward it to your customer in one tap.
Secure and private
Your quotes and client details stay in your own Google Drive account. Nobody else can access them.
30-day validity built in
Valid Until date is calculated and added automatically — exactly 30 days from the quote date.
