The Product
What you are selling
Quotomate is an automated quoting system built specifically for Australian tradies. A tradie records a 30-second voice message describing a job, emails it to their dedicated address, and receives a professional branded PDF quote in their inbox within 60 seconds — fully automatically.

The system is set up for each tradie remotely via a 90-minute Zoom call. Once live it runs automatically with zero ongoing effort from the tradie. There is no app to download, no software to install, and no monthly cost to the tradie beyond the optional $19/month support plan.

Quotomate Sales Agent Program — Terms and Conditions

1
Appointment. Quotomate (ABN 37 534 521 141) appoints the Sales Agent as a non-exclusive independent contractor to introduce potential clients to the Quotomate automated quoting system. The Sales Agent is not an employee of Quotomate and has no authority to enter into contracts on behalf of Quotomate.
2
Commission — Setup Fee. The Sales Agent earns a commission of 15% of the one-time setup fee (AU$499) for each new client they refer who completes a setup call and makes payment. This amounts to AU$74.85 per successful sale. Commission is payable within 7 business days of confirmed client payment.
3
Commission — Support Plan. Where a referred client subscribes to the optional monthly support plan (AU$19/month), the Sales Agent earns an ongoing commission of 15% (AU$2.85/month) for each month the client remains active on the support plan.
4
Referral Tracking. The Sales Agent must inform Quotomate of each referral by emailing contact@quotomate.com.au with the referred tradie's name and contact details before or at the time of booking. Commission will only be paid for referrals that are registered in advance. Quotomate reserves the right to decline a commission claim where a referral was not pre-registered.
5
No Guarantee of Sales. Quotomate makes no guarantee that referred clients will proceed with a purchase. Commission is only payable on completed, paid transactions.
6
Representations. The Sales Agent must accurately represent the Quotomate product and pricing as published on quotomate.com.au. The Sales Agent must not make any promises, guarantees, or representations about the product that are not stated on the website or in materials provided by Quotomate.
7
Pricing. The Sales Agent must not offer or negotiate pricing on behalf of Quotomate. The published price of AU$499 (setup) and AU$19/month (support) is fixed and cannot be varied by the Sales Agent.
8
Confidentiality. The Sales Agent agrees to keep confidential all information about Quotomate's systems, processes, pricing structures, and client information. This obligation continues after termination of this agreement.
9
Tax Obligations. Commission payments are made inclusive of any applicable GST. The Sales Agent is responsible for their own tax obligations arising from commission income. Quotomate will provide a summary of payments made upon request.
10
Termination. Either party may terminate this agreement at any time with 14 days written notice to contact@quotomate.com.au. Commissions earned prior to termination will be paid in full. Ongoing support plan commissions will cease upon termination.
11
Governing Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of South Australia, Australia. Any disputes will be resolved in the courts of South Australia.
